# Handshake AI Skills Studio X Google Challenge - Official Rules Summary

This document summarizes the official rules, eligibility, deadlines, and legal terms for the **Handshake AI Skills Studio X Google November Challenge**.

---

## 1. Contest Overview
- **Contest Title:** Handshake AI Skills Studio X Google *"Your Deadline, Your Design: Create a Countdown Lock Screen"*
- **Contest Sponsor:** Stryder Corp. d/b/a Handshake (225 Bush St 12th Floor, San Francisco, CA 94104) in partnership with Google.
- **Entry Fee:** Free. No purchase or payment of any kind is required to enter or win.
- **Multiple Entries:** Allowed! Participants may submit more than one project.

---

## 2. Key Dates & Deadlines
- **Submission Deadline:** **November 23, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT** (Pacific Time).
- **Winner Notification:** Within 30 days of the Entry Deadline via the email address linked to the participant's Handshake profile.

---

## 3. Eligibility Requirements
- **Age:** Must be at least 18 years old or the age of majority in your jurisdiction.
- **Account:** Must have an active Handshake account associated with the entry email address.
- **Geography:** Must be a legal resident of one of the eligible countries listed in Appendix A of the official rules.
  - **India is fully ELIGIBLE** (explicitly listed in Appendix A).
  - Other eligible countries include: United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Spain, Netherlands, Switzerland, Austria, Sweden, and others.
- **Exclusions:** Officers, directors, employees of Handshake, promotional partners, and their immediate families are not eligible.

---

## 4. Required Submission Deliverables
Every entry submitted to the mission page in the Handshake product must contain:
1. **Project Title:** A clear, memorable title for your countdown lock screen.
2. **Project Cover Image:** A visually captivating visual showing the lock screen in action.
3. **Project Description:** Explanation of the deadline, target user, and how the lock screen keeps them on track.
4. **Project Link / URL:** A live, accessible link to the project built with Google tools / mission.

---

## 5. Judging Process & Scoring Rubric (4 x 25%)

### Round 1: Scoring on 4 Weighted Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | Score 1 (Low) | Score 3 (Average) | Score 5 (Winning / Exceptional) |
|:---|:---:|:---|:---|:---|
| **Execution** | **25%** | Does not meet requirements or not formatted for phone/computer screen. | Meets all project requirements; countdown and milestones are clear. | Exceeds project requirements; ready to set as lock screen today; milestones clearly lead to deadline. |
| **Creativity** | **25%** | Common idea with minimal differentiation. | Interesting twist or thoughtful angle. | Fresh concept, unexpected use case, or particularly clever design. |
| **Usefulness / Value** | **25%** | Hard to see the point or appeal. | Clear practical or experiential value for the deadline it is built around. | Highly compelling; meaningfully keeps owner on track or delights intended audience. |
| **Polish & Thoughtfulness** | **25%** | Rough, confusing, hard to read on a lock screen. | Readable at a glance, with easy-to-follow milestones. | Feels intentional and "real", with clean space for clock, short milestones in order, style matching the deadline. |

### Round 2: Top 20 Evaluation
- The top 20 entries from Round 1 will be evaluated on the same criteria by a panel of judges from Handshake and partner sponsors to determine the final winners.

---

## 6. Prizes & Legal Conditions
- **Grand Prize:** Top **three (3) winners** will each receive **$1,000.00 US Dollars**.
- **Intellectual Property & Licensing:** Participants retain ownership of their creations, but grant Handshake and Google an irrevocable, worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free license to reproduce, publicly display, and distribute the submission for promotional purposes.
- **Governing Law:** State of California, United States.
